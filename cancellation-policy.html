IMPORTANT CANCELLATION & REFUND NOTICE

PLEASE READ THIS POLICY CAREFULLY BEFORE MAKING A RESERVATION OR SUBMITTING PAYMENT.

This Cancellation & Refund Policy governs the cancellation, modification, transfer, refund, credit, and payment-dispute conditions applicable to travel services purchased through Every Avenue Travel LLC, doing business as Worldschooling Journeys.

BY COMPLETING A BOOKING AND AFFIRMATIVELY ACCEPTING THIS POLICY, THE BOOKING CLIENT AGREES TO THE APPLICABLE CANCELLATION AND PAYMENT CONDITIONS, SUBJECT TO APPLICABLE LAW.

IMPORTANT FINANCIAL CONDITIONS

NON-REFUNDABLE DEPOSITS: Unless the applicable booking documents expressly provide otherwise, booking deposits are non-refundable from the time they are paid.

SHORE EXCURSIONS: All shore excursions purchased through Worldschooling Journeys are subject to a 100% non-refundable cancellation policy once confirmed, except as expressly provided in the applicable booking agreement or required by law.

MISSED CRUISE PORTS: If a cruise ship does not call at a scheduled port for any reason and the purchased excursion cannot operate, a 100% cancellation charge equal to the full excursion booking price applies, subject to independently binding refund commitments, the Company's own legally actionable nonperformance, and applicable law.

LAND TRIPS: Cancellation charges increase as departure approaches. Customers who cancel close to departure may forfeit the entire amount paid.

CRUISE BOOKINGS: Cruise fares, cabins, deposits, and onboard services are subject to the applicable cruise-line passenger-ticket contract and fare rules. Separately purchased Worldschooling Journeys services may have different conditions.

SUPPLIER RESTRICTIONS: Accommodations, transportation, guides, admissions, activities, permits, reservations, and other travel services may be subject to non-refundable supplier conditions.

FORCE MAJEURE: Hurricanes, severe weather, government restrictions, port closures, transportation disruptions, and other events beyond the Company's reasonable control do not automatically entitle a Traveler to a full or partial refund.

VOLUNTARY GOODWILL REFUNDS: When no refund is otherwise owed, Worldschooling Journeys may voluntarily offer a refund, credit, or other accommodation. Such accommodations are discretionary unless they become binding under a specific commitment or applicable law.

PAYMENT DISPUTES: During an active chargeback or payment dispute, the Company may temporarily hold or coordinate separate refund processing for the disputed amount to prevent duplicate reimbursement, subject to applicable law and processor requirements.

TRAVEL INSURANCE: Comprehensive travel insurance is required unless the applicable booking expressly identifies an exception.

WRITTEN CANCELLATION NOTICE: Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing using the procedures described in this Policy.

Nothing in this Policy eliminates a contractual or statutory right, refund obligation, or payment-dispute protection that cannot legally be waived.

This hierarchy does not eliminate independently binding refund commitments, remedies for actionable misrepresentations, or rights that applicable law protects.

5.How to Cancel

5.1 Written Cancellation Request

ALL CUSTOMER-REQUESTED CANCELLATIONS MUST BE SUBMITTED IN WRITING.

Cancellation requests should be emailed to:

booking@everyavenue.com

The request should clearly identify:

  • The Booking Client;
  • The booking or confirmation number, if available;
  • The Trip or excursion;
  • The scheduled departure date;
  • The Participants affected; and
  • The specific services to be canceled.

5.2 Effective Cancellation Date

The effective cancellation date is ordinarily the date the Company's designated email system receives an identifiable written request clearly communicating the intention to cancel.

The Traveler should retain proof of transmission.

The Company will not unreasonably disregard an otherwise valid cancellation notice actually received through another channel where applicable law requires recognition.

5.3 Informal Communications

Telephone calls, group-chat messages, social-media comments, or informal discussions are not the Company's preferred cancellation method.

An informal communication does not automatically constitute an accepted contractual cancellation unless the Company confirms it or applicable law requires otherwise.

5.4 Partial Cancellations

A request to cancel one Participant from a shared reservation is treated as a partial cancellation.

The remaining booking may be subject to authorized occupancy, group-pricing, or Supplier adjustments.

5.5 Transfers Are Not Cancellations

A request to transfer a reservation, substitute a Traveler, or change dates does not automatically constitute a cancellation.

Submitting a transfer request does not stop the applicable cancellation schedule.

If the Traveler subsequently decides to cancel, the effective cancellation date is determined by the actual cancellation notice.

5.6 Company Acknowledgment

The Company will review the cancellation request and determine the applicable contractual treatment.

Acknowledgment of a cancellation notice does not establish that the canceled service is refundable.

  • The date a transfer was first requested; or
  • The date the Traveler expected to receive a refund.

No refund under the standard schedule will exceed the amount properly refundable from payments actually received by the Company.

7.Land Trip Refund Calculations

7.1 Eligible Payments

For a booking governed by the standard schedule:

Eligible Payments = Payments actually received by the Company for the applicable services, minus the non-refundable Deposit and other properly excluded non-refundable amounts.

The applicable percentage in Section 6 is then applied to Eligible Payments.

7.2 Illustrative Example

Assume:

  • Total Trip price: $3,500;
  • Non-refundable Deposit: $750;
  • Total paid: $3,500;
  • Written cancellation received 100 calendar days before departure;
  • No additional applicable Supplier restriction.

The calculation would be:

ItemAmount
Total paid$3,500.00
Non-refundable Deposit$750.00
Eligible Payments$2,750.00
Applicable refund percentage75%
Calculated refund$2,062.50

This example is illustrative and does not change the terms applicable to an actual booking.

7.3 Partial Payments

Where the booking is not fully paid, the calculation is based on payments actually received, subject to amounts properly due under the Agreement.

Cancellation does not automatically eliminate:

  • An installment already due;
  • Valid Supplier obligations already incurred;
  • Properly disclosed non-refundable payment obligations; or
  • Authorized expenses resulting from Traveler-requested changes.

7.4 No Duplicate Deductions

The same expense, cancellation charge, or payment adjustment will not be counted twice.

Separate Supplier cancellation conditions will be applied only to the components they validly govern.

7.5 Accounting and Documentation

Refund calculations may consider:

  • The original transaction;
  • Deposits;
  • Applicable cancellation percentages;
  • Services already delivered;
  • Non-refundable Supplier commitments;
  • Authorized expenses;
  • Funds properly refundable under the Agreement;
  • Supplier recoveries;
  • Previous refunds;
  • Company Credits;

7.6 Internal Commercial Information

The Company may provide a reasonable summary of an applicable refund calculation without disclosing confidential wholesale Supplier rates, internal pricing arrangements, or profit margins unless disclosure is required by the governing Agreement or law.

7.7 Other Refund Methods

The standard land-trip calculation does not automatically apply to shore excursions, cruises, or bookings governed by other accepted cancellation conditions.

Reinstatement of a canceled reservation is not guaranteed.

For reservations made after the final-payment deadline, the full booking balance may be payable immediately.

A family required to depart because of substantiated serious child-supervision or safety concerns is subject to the applicable removal and refund provisions.

12.7 Company Nonperformance

This Section does not eliminate a remedy arising from the Company's own material failure to deliver an agreed service.
