After years of bringing worldschooling families together, here is what we have learned: many of us are craving real connection, but we have been conditioned to think it has to be complicated, expensive, or perfectly orchestrated to work.

That is exactly backwards.

Our worldschooling trips work precisely because we've stripped away all the unnecessary stuff that gets in the way of what actually matters: homeschooling families connecting with other homeschooling families who get it.

Two Different Ways to Worldschool Together

Our land trips and cruises are intentionally different because families do not all want the same kind of travel experience.

Some families want carefully planned days, hands-on cultural experiences, dedicated trip leaders, and the freedom to enjoy a destination without carrying the mental load of organizing everything themselves.

Others want a more flexible experience where they can create their own rhythm, choose how much they participate, and connect naturally while the cruise ship takes care of accommodation, meals, entertainment, and daily activities.

Neither experience is better than the other.

They simply serve different families, seasons of life, budgets, destinations, and travel personalities.

What Every Trip Has in Common

Whichever way you travel with us, you're stepping into an experience where the stressful parts of family travel have already been handled.

What we add is the thing missing from most family travel: other worldschooling and homeschooling families who understand why you chose this unconventional path, who won't judge your kids for being curious instead of quiet, and who actually want to talk about something deeper than the weather.

The connection starts before you ever leave home. For every cruise and land trip, we create a WhatsApp group and a family connection document so you can get to know each other ahead of time, find the families your kids will click with, and stay in touch long after the trip ends. All at no additional cost.

The formats are different, but the heart of the experience is the same: families exploring the world together and finding a community that makes travel feel completely different.

Our Worldschooling Cruises

Flexible, community-driven trips: the ship provides the structure and activities, we help create the connections.

What a Cruise Looks Like

On our cruises, the ship takes care of the logistics that usually overwhelm parents: accommodation, food, entertainment, safe spaces for kids, and activities throughout the day.

You don't need to research restaurants, plan every hour, or figure out how to entertain your children for 12 hours straight. The structure is already there.

Our team helps facilitate meetups, group dining, and opportunities to connect, but families are free to shape their own rhythm. Some spend every meal together. Others connect during sea days and enjoy family time at the ports. Some kids become inseparable, while others drift naturally between friend groups and enjoy having a large group of friends to go feral with.

Our cruises are flexible, community-led experiences where families choose how much they participate while remaining part of something bigger.

How We Price Our Cruises

We carefully select 30–40 sailings each year from thousands of options, choosing the best value, most exciting itineraries, and family-friendly ships. We then offer them to our community at the exact same price charged by the cruise line.

No markup. No group leader fee. No hidden community cost.

If we added the same level of hands-on guidance as our land trips, cruise prices would increase significantly, often by $500–$1,000 per cabin. That would make them inaccessible to many of the families who need this community most.

So we provide just enough structure to help families connect, then let the magic happen naturally.

Everything Included with Your Cruise, At No Extra Cost

Just because this isn't a guided experience doesn't mean you're figuring it out alone. Our 10-person team spends months setting up each sailing and worldschooling cruise community:

  • Pre-cruise WhatsApp group and family connection document so families can connect before you even leave home
  • Shared hotel options for the nights before and after sailing
  • Pre-cruise meetup coordination so you actually put the faces to the names and find each other on the ship amongst 4000 passengers
  • Onboard meet and greet to get to know each other better and help the kids (and adults) connect
  • Educational ship's crew meeting where the staff teaches the kids and adults what it's like to run a ship (subject to ship's approval)
  • Group dining in a dedicated section of the restaurant for families to re-connect every night
  • Onboard group coordinator to help reserve conference rooms or find venues to meet at (subject to ship's approval)
  • Educational shore excursions (private for our group only), specially designed for our worldschooling families (this way you don't have to shush your kids because they are bothering the older cruise demographic on the tour bus, we get to explore privately. The families all get it and let the kids be kids)
  • A complete cruise guide with practical tips for building community onboard
  • 24/7 support for any questions, concerns, or last-minute changes

This way we focus on creating the framework for connection rather than managing each day's experience.

The Messy, Beautiful Reality

We are going to be honest about something: our cruises can feel overwhelming at first, especially if you are used to traveling only with your own family or prefer more structured experiences.

You might board the ship wondering if you will actually connect with anyone. You might feel awkward joining conversations or uncertain about which activities to choose. You may wonder how you will fit in.

That is normal. Building real connections is not Instagram-perfect from day one.

But what we have seen over and over is this: within a few days, kids are running around in groups. Parents are planning their next trip together. By the end, families are exchanging travel plans and making commitments to meet again.

The families who engage with the community (who join the chats, show up to meetups, initiate get-togethers, say yes to shared dinners) consistently have the richest journeys. They make the most friends, find their "soul family," learn from others, and often say things like, "I did not think this was possible, but we found OUR PEOPLE!"

Those who stay in their cabins, keep to themselves, or avoid group activities often wish they had participated more. You really do get out of the community what you put into it.

We have seen autistic children break out of their shells and become the life of the party; the shyest child step up and sing karaoke in front of everyone; and a boy who arrived hunched over and reserved stand taller and more confidently by the end.

We have heard children say they never thought it was possible to have friends who did not judge or exclude them. We have seen families travel across the country to reunite, move states to live closer to one another, and plan years of future adventures together.

So many happy endings… or perhaps we should say beginnings.

Who Our Worldschooling Cruises Are Actually For

Our worldschooling cruises work best for homeschooling families who:

  • Crave community but also value their independence
  • Want their kids to experience deep friendship with other worldschooling children
  • Are ready to let go of controlling every detail and trust the process
  • Understand that the best connections happen organically, not on schedule
  • Want to feel part of something meaningful without paying premium prices
  • Want their children to have a taste of "The Millennial Childhood" experience
  • Are looking for life-long friends who don't question their choices

Our worldschooling cruises are not guided group tours. They are flexible, community-driven experiences built around the fact that the cruise ship already provides the structure and activities, while we help create the connections around it.

That is what allows families to travel at the cruise line's regular rates, join a worldschooling community, and shape the experience in the way that works best for them.

Our Worldschooling Land Trips

Immersive, planned trips with a full schedule of activities, led by retreat leaders from the worldschooling community.

What a Land Trip Looks Like

On our worldschooling land trips, our team takes care of the itinerary, activities, and many of the daily details. Each trip is led by retreat leaders from the worldschooling community, and depending on the destination, we bring in local guides for specific tours and experiences. Instead of spending your trip coordinating logistics, you can focus on exploring, learning, and experiencing the destination alongside your children.

Families share more of the daily experience here. You might explore ancient sites together, ride horses, hike through the mountains, join local tours, try traditional food, or spend long bus rides talking and laughing with people who were strangers just days before. The itinerary is planned and organized, but the friendships and unexpected moments develop naturally.

Every land trip has its own shape. Some, like Austria, are based in one place, so families settle in and really get to know the area. Others, like Morocco and the Galápagos, move between several locations, so you see more of the country along the way.

Our land trips are more immersive and structured, with educational experiences designed to help families connect deeply with both the destination and one another.

How We Price Our Land Trips

Our land trips are different by design. They include hands-on planning, retreat leaders, and daily coordination, with local tours and guides built in where the destination calls for it. The pricing reflects that more immersive, supported experience. Some of the itineraries take more than one year to perfect.

What's Included in Our Land Trips

Because every land trip is built around its destination, the details vary from trip to trip. You'll find the full list on each trip's page. Every land trip includes:

  • Accommodation for the whole trip
  • Dedicated trip support before, during, and after your journey
  • A WhatsApp group and family connection document so you can get to know the other families before you go and stay in touch afterward, at no additional cost

On trips that move between locations, like Morocco and the Galápagos, transportation between destinations is included too.

Who Might Love Our Land Trips

If you thrive with carefully curated daily experiences and dedicated leaders creating magical moments throughout your journey, our land-based Worldschooling Journeys trips are absolutely incredible.

Those trips feature retreat leaders from our worldschooling community, local tours and guides where the destination calls for it, immersive cultural experiences, and hands-on support that creates deep educational opportunities in stunning destinations, often moving faster, exploring more and more in depth. Both styles are beautiful. They just serve different travel personalities and family needs.

Our worldschooling land trips are designed to have an immersive community travel experience where you get to feel the destination, not just see it. And do it with a group of other amazing worldschoolers who will quickly become life-long friends. Our company's mission is to combine travel and community, because in the end, it's not where you go to, it's who you travel there with.
