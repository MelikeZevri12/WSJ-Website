5.5 Community Volunteers, Community Hosts, and Trip Leadership

Worldschooling Journeys organizes group cruises, land trips, and other travel experiences designed to bring families together, encourage meaningful connections, and create opportunities for shared cultural, educational, and social experiences.

Community Volunteers: Existing, booked Participants who voluntarily offer occasional, informal community assistance without receiving compensation, travel discounts, or other financial benefits in exchange for that assistance.

Community Hosts: Individuals selected by Worldschooling Journeys who receive a specified travel or accommodation discount in consideration for agreed community-hosting or Trip coordination responsibilities.

These roles have different selection processes, compensation arrangements, responsibilities, and levels of authority.

The specific responsibilities and authority of a Community Host depend on whether the individual is serving on a cruise or land Trip.

Unless otherwise expressly stated in the applicable Trip Documents, neither role includes childcare, professional medical services, security services, or technical activity instruction.

The presence of a Community Volunteer or Community Host does not eliminate the services or responsibilities expressly undertaken by Worldschooling Journeys under the applicable booking Agreement.

5.5.2 Community Volunteers: Selection and Uncompensated Participation

Community Volunteers are invited from among families who have already booked and confirmed their participation in a Worldschooling Journeys cruise or land Trip.

After the participating families have been identified, Worldschooling Journeys may invite one or more adult Participants to assist informally with community introductions, social connections, and group engagement.

Participation is entirely optional and is not a condition of purchasing or participating in a Trip.

Community Volunteers:

  • Are already participating travelers.
  • Do not receive wages, stipends, cash payments, travel discounts, complimentary accommodations, or other financial benefits in exchange for their informal assistance.
  • Are not assigned mandatory work shifts or operational responsibilities.
  • Are not required to provide continuous assistance.
  • Are not responsible for delivering the Company's contracted services.
  • May participate according to their interests and availability.
  • May decline requests or discontinue their informal participation.

Their activities may include welcoming families, facilitating introductions, suggesting social gatherings, sharing general information already supplied by the Company, and encouraging community participation.

Community Volunteers have no authority to make contractual, financial, operational, or safety decisions on behalf of Worldschooling Journeys solely by virtue of this informal role.

5.5.3 Community Hosts: Selection and Discounted Travel Arrangements

Community Hosts are individuals selected by Worldschooling Journeys to assist with agreed community-building, hosting, or Trip coordination responsibilities.

Unlike Community Volunteers, Community Hosts receive a specified travel-related discount or accommodation benefit in consideration for their agreed responsibilities.

Depending on the Trip, the arrangement may include:

  • A discounted cruise booking or cruise fare.
  • A reduced hotel or accommodation charge.
  • A discount on a designated component of a land Trip.
  • Another specifically documented partial travel or accommodation discount.

Worldschooling Journeys does not ordinarily provide Community Hosts with completely complimentary cruises, fully funded land Trips, or payment of all personal travel expenses.

Unless their separate written arrangement provides otherwise, Community Hosts remain responsible for their remaining booking balance, airfare, travel insurance, personal transportation, optional excursions, personal purchases, and other expenses not expressly included.

Community Hosts do not receive a cash salary under the intended hosting arrangement. However, the agreed discount constitutes a form of noncash compensation or consideration.

The Community Host designation does not, by itself, determine the individual's legal employment, independent contractor, or agency status. Those matters are governed by the actual relationship and applicable law.

  • Changing meeting or departure times.
  • Adjusting the order of scheduled activities.
  • Allowing additional time at an attraction.
  • Shortening an activity because of weather, fatigue, or other reasonable considerations.
  • Moving an activity to a different time when practical.
  • Adjusting schedules because of transportation delays.
  • Changing meeting locations when necessary.
  • Coordinating reasonable alternative activities when an original arrangement becomes impractical.

  2. The practical needs of participating families.

  4. The availability of guides, suppliers, and scheduled services.
