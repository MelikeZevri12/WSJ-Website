The Healing Gallery

What Makes the Healing Gallery Unique

01

A Unique Therapeutic Climate

Deep inside the Radhausberg mountain, the Healing Gallery combines naturally occurring radon with temperatures of 37–41.5°C (98.6–106.7°F) and humidity of 75–100%. This combination is a globally unique healing climate.

02

A Tradition Since 1952

The therapeutic climate inside the mountain has been used since 1952. Today, the Healing Gallery operates as a medical therapy centre, with each participant’s treatment guided individually by its medical professionals.

03

The Therapy Experience

Guests travel approximately 2.5 kilometres into the mountain by train before resting at designated therapy stations in the warm, humid environment. After returning from the mountain, the Healing Gallery recommends approximately 30 minutes of relaxation.

04

A Multi-Session Experience

The Healing Gallery generally recommends 8–12 sessions over 2–3 weeks, although the appropriate program is determined individually. Our three-week retreat gives guests the time to experience their recommended sessions without rushing their stay.

The Healing Gallery

Why people come to the Healing Gallery

The Healing Gallery is not a one-time spa treatment. People come for a structured course of therapy, often over several weeks, with the aim of improving symptoms associated with chronic inflammatory and rheumatic conditions.

A typical course may involve 8–12 visits over 2–3 weeks, depending on the individual and the recommendations of the Healing Gallery’s medical team.

Your Health, Your Family

Focus on your health, without putting family life on hold.

One of the biggest reasons we created this retreat is to give parents the time and space to actually focus on their own health.

While you attend Healing Gallery sessions, rest between treatments, use the thermal facilities, or simply take a slower day, your children can spend time with other worldschooling kids through supervised Kids Club activities.

The therapy focuses on the symptoms that can have the biggest impact on everyday life. Depending on your condition, that may mean pain, inflammation, mobility, fatigue, or overall wellbeing.

Pain & mobility

The Healing Gallery therapy is used with the aim of helping reduce pain and inflammation and supporting easier movement, especially for people living with rheumatic and chronic pain conditions.

Fatigue & overall wellbeing

For some people, the goal is not only pain relief, but also feeling less physically drained and improving overall wellbeing. This is especially relevant for conditions where fatigue is a major part of daily life.

Medication needs

Some people report needing less pain medication following a treatment course. Any changes to medication should always be made together with the doctor who manages your care.

Who Is the Healing Gallery For?

Conditions treated at the Healing Gallery

The Healing Gallery treats a range of chronic inflammatory, rheumatic, respiratory, skin, and pain conditions. Their doctors assess every person individually before treatment begins.

01

Allergies

Allergies are one of the respiratory and immune-related conditions treated at the Healing Gallery. The therapy is used with the aim of reducing inflammation and supporting symptom relief, depending on your individual health situation.

02

Rheumatoid arthritis

The Healing Gallery therapy is used to support people dealing with ongoing joint pain, inflammation, stiffness, and reduced movement. The aim is symptom relief and better day-to-day function, rather than treating the underlying cause of the disease.

03

Fibromyalgia

For fibromyalgia, the focus is often on widespread pain, muscle tension, fatigue, and overall wellbeing. The heat, humidity, and radon therapy are used as part of a treatment course designed to reduce symptom burden.

04

Bronchial asthma

Asthma is one of the respiratory conditions treated at the Healing Gallery. The aim is to help manage ongoing symptoms, with the doctors assessing each person individually before treatment.

05

Chronic sinusitis

People with chronic sinusitis also come to the Healing Gallery as part of its respiratory treatment program, with the goal of improving ongoing symptoms and overall wellbeing.

06

COPD

COPD is included among the respiratory conditions treated at the Healing Gallery. Because every case is different, the doctors assess each person individually before recommending a treatment plan.

07

Psoriasis & atopic dermatitis

Psoriasis and atopic dermatitis are among the skin conditions treated at the Healing Gallery. The therapy focuses on symptom relief and inflammation, with the doctors also taking your individual health and heat tolerance into account.

08

Ankylosing spondylitis

One of the rheumatic conditions treated at the Healing Gallery. The therapy is used to help with pain, inflammation, stiffness, and mobility, with some people experiencing benefits that continue beyond the treatment period.

Other conditions the Healing Gallery also treats

The Healing Gallery also works with people living with osteoarthritis, osteoporosis, degenerative spinal and joint conditions, sarcoidosis, and other chronic pain conditions.

The therapy is focused on helping with symptoms and improving day-to-day wellbeing, rather than curing the underlying condition. Before starting, you’ll meet with the Healing Gallery’s medical team so they can review your health, make sure the therapy is appropriate for you, and recommend the right course of treatment.

Inside the Mountain

What a Healing Gallery session actually looks like

Once you’ve been medically cleared and have your treatment plan, a small train takes you around two kilometres into the Radhausberg mountain. Inside, different therapy stations combine naturally occurring radon, heat, and high humidity, with the setting chosen for you based on your individual health and heat tolerance.

01

Travel into the mountain

You board the small gallery train and travel deep into the mountain to your assigned therapy station.

02

Rest at your therapy station

Once there, you spend about an hour resting in the warm, humid environment while the treatment takes place.

03

Give yourself time to recover

After leaving the mountain, the Healing Gallery recommends around 30 minutes of rest. That recovery time is part of the treatment rhythm, not something you have to rush through.

Salzburgerland

Nature’s Healing Elements

Healing Mountain Air

Step outside into the fresh air of the Austrian Alps, surrounded by mountains, forests, and wide-open landscapes. Whether walking through the valley or simply slowing down on a quiet afternoon, the Alpine setting becomes part of the experience.

Healing Thermal Waters

Naturally warm thermal water rises from deep within the mountains and supplies the valley’s renowned thermal spas. Bathing in these waters has been part of the valley’s wellness tradition for centuries and remains one of the defining experiences of a stay here.

Healing Waterfall

Flowing through the heart of town, the waterfall is one of the valley’s most striking natural landmarks. Its powerful cascade, cool mist, and surrounding mountain setting create a refreshing place to walk, pause, and experience the landscape up close.

Healing Springs

The area’s natural springs are deeply connected to the valley’s history as a spa destination. For generations, visitors have traveled here to experience its thermal waters, and today those springs remain at the heart of the region’s bathing and wellness culture.

Spa Culture

Pools & Whirlpool

    Before Treatment

    A few important things to know before treatment

    The Healing Gallery is a controlled medical setting, and every person is assessed before beginning therapy. Because the treatment involves radon, heat, and high humidity, it’s important to discuss it with your doctor and make sure it’s appropriate for your individual health situation.

    Radon is radioactive, and prolonged indoor exposure is associated with lung-cancer risk. The exposure inside the Healing Gallery is time-limited and medically supervised, so this is something to weigh together with your doctor based on your diagnosis, treatment history, smoking exposure, medications, and overall health.

    The Healing Gallery treatment is arranged directly with their medical team and is separate from your retreat booking.

    When the therapy may not be suitable

    The Healing Gallery advises against treatment during pregnancy and with untreated hyperthyroidism. They also recommend waiting at least one year after cancer treatment.

    If you have severe cardiovascular or kidney disease, an acute infection, claustrophobia, or difficulty tolerating heat and humidity, speak with the Healing Gallery doctors before planning treatment.

    And if you take prescribed medication, any changes should always be made together with the doctor who manages your care.
